# KI-GE-21

> A quadrilateral is a closed figure in a plane bounded by four line segments, in which adjacent sides meet only at their common end point, opposite sides do not meet and no three vertices lie on one straight line; it has four sides, four distinct vertices and four interior angles.

Kind: Concept. Where required, it is required **before teaching**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 6.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. NCERT (2017) places quadrilaterals in Grade 6 (Understanding Elementary Shapes); not checked against the textbook. Statement revised 11 Nov 2026 (Bijit's decision that the sides do not cross; wording checked by ChatGPT, bridge request cd28102b): it excludes crossed shapes, opposite sides that touch and three vertices in a line, and allows concave quadrilaterals.
