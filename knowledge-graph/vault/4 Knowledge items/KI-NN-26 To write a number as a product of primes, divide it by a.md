# KI-NN-26

> To write a number as a product of primes, divide it by a prime that divides it, repeat on each quotient until the quotient is 1, and write the divisors as a product.

Kind: Procedure. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 6.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. A factor tree gives the same result; it is a way of recording the same procedure.
