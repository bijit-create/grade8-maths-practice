# KI-PR-12

> When all the outcomes of an experiment are equally likely, the theoretical probability of an event is the number of outcomes in the event divided by the total number of outcomes.

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 7.

**Note.** Two kind readings: Principle (states what is true under equal likelihood) or Procedure (count and divide); Principle chosen, gate before attainment.
