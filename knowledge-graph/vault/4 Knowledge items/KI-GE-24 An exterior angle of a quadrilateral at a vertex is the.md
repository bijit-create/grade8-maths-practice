# KI-GE-24

> An exterior angle of a quadrilateral at a vertex is the angle between one side at that vertex and the extension of the adjacent side, so it and the interior angle at that vertex together make a straight line.

Kind: Concept. Where required, it is required **before teaching**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. NCERT (2017) Grade 7 has the exterior angle of a triangle; Grade 8 extends the term to polygons.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
