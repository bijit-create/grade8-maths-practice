# KI-PR-22

> In repeated trials of an experiment with equally likely outcomes, the number of times an event happens is usually close to, but seldom exactly equal to, the number of trials times the theoretical probability of the event.

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 8.
