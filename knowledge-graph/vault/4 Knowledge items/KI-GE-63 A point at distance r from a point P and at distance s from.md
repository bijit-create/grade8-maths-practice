# KI-GE-63

> A point at distance r from a point P and at distance s from a point Q lies on the circle with centre P and radius r and on the circle with centre Q and radius s, so it is a point where the two circles meet.

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 7.
