# KI-NN-33

> To find the square root of a whole number by the division method, group its digits in pairs from the right; take as the first digit of the root the largest digit whose square is not more than the first group, subtract, bring down the next pair, and choose each next digit so that the new divisor, which is twice the root so far followed by that digit, multiplied by that digit is not more than the remainder; repeat until no pairs are left.

Kind: Procedure. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 8.
