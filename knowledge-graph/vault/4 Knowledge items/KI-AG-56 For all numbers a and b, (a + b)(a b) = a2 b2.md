# KI-AG-56

> For all numbers a and b, (a + b)(a − b) = a² − b².

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. Read from right to left, it is the factorisation of a difference of two squares; the numerical use in SK-AG-41 is the only right-to-left use in this graph.
