# KI-NN-34

> To find the square root of a decimal by the division method, group the digits of the whole-number part in pairs leftwards from the decimal point and the digits of the decimal part in pairs rightwards, adding a zero to complete the last pair, divide as for a whole number, and write the decimal point in the root when the first pair after the decimal point is brought down.
