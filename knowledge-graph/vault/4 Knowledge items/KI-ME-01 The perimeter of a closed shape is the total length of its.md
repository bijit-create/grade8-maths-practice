# KI-ME-01

> The perimeter of a closed shape is the total length of its boundary, measured in units of length.

Kind: Concept. Where required, it is required **before teaching**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 6.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. NCERT (2017) LO-G6-17 places perimeter in Grade 6.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
