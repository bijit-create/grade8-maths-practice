# KI-DA-34

> A pie chart is a circle divided into sectors by radii, in which the whole circle stands for the total of the data and each sector shows the share of one category, with a central angle proportional to that share.

Kind: Concept. Where required, it is required **before teaching**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** Also called a circle graph in the NCERT Class 8 chapter.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
