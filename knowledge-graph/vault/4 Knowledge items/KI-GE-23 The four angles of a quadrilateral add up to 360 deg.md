# KI-GE-23

> The four angles of a quadrilateral add up to 360°.

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. The reason is part of the Grade 8 learning: a diagonal splits a convex quadrilateral into two triangles, each with angle sum 180°. The skills in this graph use convex quadrilaterals only.
