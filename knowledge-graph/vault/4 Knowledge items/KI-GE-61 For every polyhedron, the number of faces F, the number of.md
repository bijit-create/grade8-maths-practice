# KI-GE-61

> For every polyhedron, the number of faces F, the number of vertices V and the number of edges E satisfy F + V − E = 2.

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. Euler's relation as in NCERT (2017) Grade 8 (Visualising Solid Shapes, removed from the textbook in 2023). The textbook checks it on a table of solids; a proof is not part of the learning.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
