# KI-NN-21

> A whole number is divisible by 9 exactly when the sum of its digits is divisible by 9.

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 6.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. A divisibility rule. NCERT Class 6 states the test; the Class 8 outcome (LO-M803) asks for its proof. Kind has two readings (Principle, because it states what is true of every whole number, or Procedure, because it is used as a test); recorded as Principle.
