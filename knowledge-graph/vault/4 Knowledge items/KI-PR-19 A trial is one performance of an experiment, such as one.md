# KI-PR-19

> A trial is one performance of an experiment, such as one toss of a coin or one throw of a die, and an event happens in a trial when the result is one of the outcomes of the event.

Kind: Concept. Where required, it is required **before teaching**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** The Class 8 chapter speaks of tossing a coin or throwing a die a number of times; the word trial is used in the Class 9 chapter. Replaces the simulation wording of MYP2 KI-PR-16, which is not copied.
