# KI-ME-31

> The lateral surface area of a cuboid with length l, width w and height h is 2h(l + w), the perimeter of its base multiplied by its height.

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. The cube is the case with l = w = h, through KI-GE-20; no separate cube item is written.
