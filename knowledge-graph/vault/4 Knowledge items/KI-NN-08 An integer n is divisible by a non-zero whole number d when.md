# KI-NN-08

> An integer n is divisible by a non-zero whole number d when n = d × k for some integer k; for whole numbers this means n is a multiple of d and d is a factor of n.

Kind: Concept. Where required, it is required **before teaching**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 6.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. NCERT Class 6 (Playing with Numbers) uses divisibility of whole numbers; the integer form is stated here because the rule of 11 produces a remainder expression that can be negative.
