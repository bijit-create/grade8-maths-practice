# KI-ME-02

> The area of a shape is the amount of surface it covers, measured in square units, where 1 cm² is the area of a square with sides of 1 cm and 1 m² is the area of a square with sides of 1 m.

Kind: Concept. Where required, it is required **before teaching**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 6.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. NCERT (2017) LO-G6-17 places area in Grade 6.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
