# KI-ME-18

> The volume of a solid is the amount of space it occupies, measured in cubic units, where 1 cm³ is the volume of a cube with edges of 1 cm and 1 m³ is the volume of a cube with edges of 1 m.

Kind: Concept. Where required, it is required **before teaching**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. MYP2 expects it in Grade 7; NCERT (2017) places volume of a cuboid in Grade 8 (M818).

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
