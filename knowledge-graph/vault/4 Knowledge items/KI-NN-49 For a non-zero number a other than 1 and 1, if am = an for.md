# KI-NN-49

> For a non-zero number a other than 1 and −1, if a^m = a^n for integers m and n, then m = n.

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. Used without statement in the NCERT Class 8 exercises that ask for an unknown exponent.
