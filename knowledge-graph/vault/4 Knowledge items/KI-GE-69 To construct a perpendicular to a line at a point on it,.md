# KI-GE-69

> To construct a perpendicular to a line at a point on it, mark two points on the line at equal distances from that point, draw from each an arc of the same larger radius so that the arcs cross, and join the crossing point to the given point.

Kind: Procedure. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 6.
