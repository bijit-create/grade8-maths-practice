# KI-GE-74

> To construct a parallelogram ABCD from sides AB and AD and angle A, draw AB, draw angle A at A and mark D on its arm at distance AD, then mark C as the point at distance AD from B and at distance AB from D.

Kind: Procedure. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. The step that finds C uses the equality of opposite sides (KI-GE-13). NCERT (2017) Grade 8 (Practical Geometry, removed from the textbook in 2023).

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
