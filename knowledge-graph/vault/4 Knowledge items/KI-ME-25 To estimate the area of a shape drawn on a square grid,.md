# KI-ME-25

> To estimate the area of a shape drawn on a square grid, count each full square and each square covered by more than half as 1, count each square covered by half as one half, leave out each square covered by less than half, and add; the total is the area in square units.

Kind: Procedure. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 6.
