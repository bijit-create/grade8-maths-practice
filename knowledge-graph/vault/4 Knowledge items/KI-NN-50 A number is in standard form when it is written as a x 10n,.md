# KI-NN-50

> A number is in standard form when it is written as a × 10^n, where 1 ≤ a < 10 and n is an integer.

Kind: Concept. Where required, it is required **before teaching**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. NCERT Class 7 gives standard form for large numbers (n positive); Class 8 adds small numbers (n negative).

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
