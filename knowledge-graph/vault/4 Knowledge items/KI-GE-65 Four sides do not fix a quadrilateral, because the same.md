# KI-GE-65

> Four sides do not fix a quadrilateral, because the same four sides can be joined to give quadrilaterals of different shapes, so a fifth measurement such as a diagonal or an angle is needed.

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. NCERT (2017) Grade 8 (Practical Geometry, removed from the textbook in 2023) gives this as the reason a diagonal or an angle is added.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
