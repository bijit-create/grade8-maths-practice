# KI-GE-67

> To draw an angle of a given measure at a point on a line with a protractor, place the centre of the protractor on the point and its base line along the line, mark the given measure on the scale, and draw the ray from the point through the mark.

Kind: Procedure. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 6.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. NCERT (2017) Grade 6 (Understanding Elementary Shapes) measures and draws angles with a protractor.
