# KI-NN-11

> A two-digit number with tens digit a and units digit b is 10a + b, and a three-digit number with hundreds digit a, tens digit b and units digit c is 100a + 10b + c.

Kind: Concept. Where required, it is required **before teaching**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. Generalised form of a number, NCERT Class 8 Playing with Numbers. Recorded as a Concept because the form is the meaning of writing digits side by side; a Procedure reading (write the number as 10a + b) is possible.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
