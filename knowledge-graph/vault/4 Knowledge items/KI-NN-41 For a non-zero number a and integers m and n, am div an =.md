# KI-NN-41

> For a non-zero number a and integers m and n, a^m ÷ a^n = a^(m − n).

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. NCERT Class 7 states this law for whole-number exponents; Class 8 extends it to integer exponents, which is how it is stated here.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
