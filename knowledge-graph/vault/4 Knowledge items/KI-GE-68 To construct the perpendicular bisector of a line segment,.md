# KI-GE-68

> To construct the perpendicular bisector of a line segment, draw from each end point an arc of the same radius, longer than half the segment, so that the arcs cross on both sides of the segment, and join the two crossing points.

Kind: Procedure. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 6.
