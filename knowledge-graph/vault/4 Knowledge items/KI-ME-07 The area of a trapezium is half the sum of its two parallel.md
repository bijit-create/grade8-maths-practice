# KI-ME-07

> The area of a trapezium is half the sum of its two parallel sides multiplied by the perpendicular distance between them.

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. MYP2 expects it in Grade 7; NCERT (2017) places the area of a trapezium in Grade 8 (M816).

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
