# KI-GE-53

> To draw an isometric sketch of a cuboid on isometric dot paper, draw its edges along the vertical dot lines and the two sets of slanting dot lines, each edge as many dot spaces long as the number of units in its length.

Kind: Procedure. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 7.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. NCERT (2017) places isometric sketches in Grade 7 (Visualising Solid Shapes); the chapter was removed from the textbook in 2023.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
