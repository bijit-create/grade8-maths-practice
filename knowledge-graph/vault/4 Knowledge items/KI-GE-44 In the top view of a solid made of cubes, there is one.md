# KI-GE-44

> In the top view of a solid made of cubes, there is one square for each stack of cubes, whatever the height of the stack.

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. The reason is that the top view shows only what is seen from above, which is the top of each stack.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
