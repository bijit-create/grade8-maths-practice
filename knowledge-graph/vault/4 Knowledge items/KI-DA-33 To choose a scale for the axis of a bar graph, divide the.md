# KI-DA-33

> To choose a scale for the axis of a bar graph, divide the length of the axis into equal divisions that each stand for a convenient number of units, such as 1, 2, 5, 10 or 100, so that the greatest value fits on the axis.

Kind: Procedure. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 6.

**Note.** Two kind readings: Procedure (choose, then check that the greatest value fits) or Principle (a scale is usable when its divisions are equal and the greatest value fits); Procedure chosen, gate before attainment.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
