# KI-GE-45

> In the front view or a side view of a solid made of cubes, each position across the view has a column with as many squares as there are cubes in the tallest stack in line with the viewer at that position.

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. The reason is that the cubes behind the tallest stack in line with the viewer are hidden by it.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
