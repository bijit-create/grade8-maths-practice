# KI-AG-52

> To multiply two polynomials, multiply every term of the first by every term of the second, keeping each sign, and add the products.

Kind: Procedure. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. The reason is the distributive property applied to each term of the first polynomial in turn. The sum of the products is collected by KI-AG-11 and KI-AG-12.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
