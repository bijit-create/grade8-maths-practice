# KI-GE-39

> A rectangle is a square exactly when two adjacent sides are equal in length, and a rhombus is a square exactly when one angle is a right angle.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. The reason follows from the definitions and from the properties of a parallelogram.
