# KI-AG-02

> A number written directly before a letter is multiplied by it, so 3x means 3 × x, x means 1 × x, -x means -1 × x and xy means x × y.

Kind: Fact or term. Where required, it is required **before teaching**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 6.
