# KI-ME-33

> The curved surface area of a cylinder with base radius r and height h is 2πrh, because the curved surface opens out into a rectangle as long as the circumference of the base and as wide as the height.

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026.
