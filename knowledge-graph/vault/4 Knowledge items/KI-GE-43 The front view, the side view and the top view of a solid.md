# KI-GE-43

> The front view, the side view and the top view of a solid are the plane figures seen when the solid is looked at from the front, from a side and from above.

Kind: Concept. Where required, it is required **before teaching**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. NCERT (2017) places views of solids in Grade 8 (Visualising Solid Shapes, removed from the textbook in 2023).
