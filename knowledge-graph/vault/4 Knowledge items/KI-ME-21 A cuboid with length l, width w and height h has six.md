# KI-ME-21

> A cuboid with length l, width w and height h has six rectangular faces in three pairs of equal opposite faces, so its total surface area is 2(lw + wh + lh).

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. MYP2 expects it in Grade 7; NCERT (2017) places it in Grade 8 (M818).
