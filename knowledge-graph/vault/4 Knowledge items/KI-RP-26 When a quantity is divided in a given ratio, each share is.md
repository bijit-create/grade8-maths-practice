# KI-RP-26

> When a quantity is divided in a given ratio, each share is the quantity multiplied by that share's term divided by the sum of the terms.

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 7.

**Note.** Two readings of kind: as a statement of what each share is (principle) or as the steps divide by the sum of the terms, then multiply (procedure); recorded as principle.
