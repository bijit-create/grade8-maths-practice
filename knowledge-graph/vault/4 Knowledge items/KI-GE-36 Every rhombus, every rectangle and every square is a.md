# KI-GE-36

> Every rhombus, every rectangle and every square is a parallelogram, so each has every property of a parallelogram.

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. The reason is the definitions: a rhombus and a rectangle are each a parallelogram with one extra condition, and a square is a rectangle.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
