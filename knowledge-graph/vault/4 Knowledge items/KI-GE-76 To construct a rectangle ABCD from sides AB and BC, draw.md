# KI-GE-76

> To construct a rectangle ABCD from sides AB and BC, draw AB, construct perpendiculars to AB at A and at B, mark D on the perpendicular at A and C on the perpendicular at B, each at distance BC from AB on the same side of AB, and join C and D; when AB equals BC the rectangle is a square.
