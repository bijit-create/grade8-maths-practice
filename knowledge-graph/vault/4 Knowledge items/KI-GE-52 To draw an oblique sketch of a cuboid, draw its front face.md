# KI-GE-52

> To draw an oblique sketch of a cuboid, draw its front face with its true length and height, draw an equal rectangle for the back face shifted up and to the right, and join the matching corners.

Kind: Procedure. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 7.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. NCERT (2017) places oblique sketches in Grade 7 (Visualising Solid Shapes); the chapter was removed from the textbook in 2023.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
