# KI-GE-50

> In the front view and the side view of an object made of one solid standing on the top face of another, the view of the upper solid is drawn above the view of the lower solid, and in the top view the upper solid is drawn over the lower one.

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. Holds when the upper solid rests within the top face of the lower one, which is the range used in the skills.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
