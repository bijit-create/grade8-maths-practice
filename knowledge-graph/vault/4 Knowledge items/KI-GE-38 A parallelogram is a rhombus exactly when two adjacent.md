# KI-GE-38

> A parallelogram is a rhombus exactly when two adjacent sides are equal in length, and it is a rectangle exactly when one angle is a right angle.

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. The reason: opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal, so two equal adjacent sides make all four equal; opposite angles are equal and adjacent angles add up to 180°, so one right angle makes all four right angles.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
