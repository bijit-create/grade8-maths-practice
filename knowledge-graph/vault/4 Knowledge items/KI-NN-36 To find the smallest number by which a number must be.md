# KI-NN-36

> To find the smallest number by which a number must be multiplied, or divided, to make a perfect square, write its prime factorisation and take the product of the primes that occur an odd number of times.

Kind: Procedure. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. The same product is the smallest multiplier and the smallest divisor.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
