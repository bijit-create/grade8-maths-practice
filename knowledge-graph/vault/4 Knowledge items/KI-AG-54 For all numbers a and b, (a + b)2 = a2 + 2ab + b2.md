# KI-AG-54

> For all numbers a and b, (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b².

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. NCERT shows the reason by multiplying (a + b)(a + b) and by areas.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
