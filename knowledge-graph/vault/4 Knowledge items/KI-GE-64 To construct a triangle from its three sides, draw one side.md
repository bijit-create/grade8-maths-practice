# KI-GE-64

> To construct a triangle from its three sides, draw one side as the base, draw arcs from its two end points with radii equal to the other two sides, and join the point where the arcs meet to the two end points.

Kind: Procedure. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 7.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. NCERT (2017) places construction of triangles in Grade 7.
