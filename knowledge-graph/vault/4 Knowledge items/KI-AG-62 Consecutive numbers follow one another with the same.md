# KI-AG-62

> Consecutive numbers follow one another with the same difference: 1 for consecutive whole numbers, 2 for consecutive even numbers or consecutive odd numbers, and k for consecutive multiples of k.

Kind: Concept. Where required, it is required **before teaching**. Without rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. Learners write consecutive multiples of 8 as n, n + 1 and n + 2 (the separation learner of SK-AG-25). Cross-domain (number patterns); NCERT Grade 8 Chapter 2 uses it in worked examples.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
