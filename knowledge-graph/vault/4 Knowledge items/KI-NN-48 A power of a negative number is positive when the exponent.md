# KI-NN-48

> A power of a negative number is positive when the exponent is an even integer and negative when the exponent is an odd integer.

Kind: Principle. Where required, it is required **before attainment**. With rationale. First expected in Grade 8.

**Note.** authored by ConveGenius, 24 Oct 2026. NCERT Class 7 states this for positive exponents; Class 8 uses it with negative exponents.

Required by skills in grade(s): 8.
