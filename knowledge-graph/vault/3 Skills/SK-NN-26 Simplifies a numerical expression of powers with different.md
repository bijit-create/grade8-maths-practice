# SK-NN-26

> Simplifies a numerical expression of powers with different bases and the same integer exponent, using the laws of exponents.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M805 Solves problems with integral exponents]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | simplifies |
| Content | an expression of powers with different bases and the same exponent |
| Conditions | two or three powers with different bases, integers from -10 to 10 or fractions, each with the same integer exponent from -6 to 6; combined by × and ÷; a negative base written in brackets; answer as a single power with a positive exponent, or as a number when the task says so; written symbols |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | (−3)^4 × (5/3)^4 = ?   2^(−3) × (−7)^(−3) = ? |
