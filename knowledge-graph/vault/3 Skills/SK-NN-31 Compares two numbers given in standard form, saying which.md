# SK-NN-31

> Compares two numbers given in standard form, saying which is greater.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M805 Solves problems with integral exponents]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | compares |
| Content | two positive numbers in standard form |
| Conditions | two numbers a × 10^n and b × 10^m with a and b from 1 to 9.999 and n and m integers from -20 to 15; pairs with equal exponents, and pairs with different exponents where the number with the greater coefficient has the smaller exponent; response the greater number |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Which is greater, 4.5 × 10^6 or 3.2 × 10^7?   Which is greater, 2.4 × 10^(−5) or 8 × 10^(−6)? |
