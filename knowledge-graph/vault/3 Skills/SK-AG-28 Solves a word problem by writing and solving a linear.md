# SK-AG-28

> Solves a word problem by writing and solving a linear equation in one variable that has fractions or has the variable in a denominator.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M806 Solves puzzles and daily life problems using variables]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | solves |
| Content | a word problem whose equation has fractions and reduces to a linear equation |
| Conditions | one unknown; situations of a fraction of a quantity and of what remains, a ratio of two quantities after the same change, and a rational number whose numerator and denominator are changed; denominators up to 12; the equation has the variable in numerators over number denominators, or in the numerator and denominator of a fraction equal to another fraction; the equation is not given; answer stated as the quantity asked for, with its unit, or as a rational number |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Half of a herd of deer are grazing in the field and three-fourths of the remaining are playing nearby. The rest 9 are drinking water from the pond. Find the number of deer in the herd.   The denominator of a rational number is greater than its numerator by 8. If the numerator is increased by 17 and the denominator is decreased by 1, the number obtained is 3/2. Find the rational number. |
