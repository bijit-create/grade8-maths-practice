# SK-GE-24

> Verifies Euler's relation, F + V − E = 2, for prisms and pyramids by completing a table of their faces, vertices and edges.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M814 Verifies Euler's relation through pattern]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | verifies |
| Content | Euler's relation for a set of prisms and pyramids, through the pattern in a table |
| Conditions | at least four solids from the cube, the cuboid and prisms and pyramids with a base of 3 to 6 sides; table with columns F, V, E and F + V − E; response: the completed table and a written statement of the relation the last column shows |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Complete the table for a triangular prism, a cube, a square pyramid and a hexagonal pyramid. What do you notice about F + V − E? |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Stopping rule | The counts, the value of F + V − E and the statement of what stays the same are read from one table; a table of one solid shows no pattern. |
