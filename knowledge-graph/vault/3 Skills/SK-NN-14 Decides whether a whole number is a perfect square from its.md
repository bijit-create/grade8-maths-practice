# SK-NN-14

> Decides whether a whole number is a perfect square from its prime factorisation.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M804 Finds squares, cubes and square roots and cube roots]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | decides |
| Content | whether a whole number is a perfect square |
| Conditions | whole numbers from 100 to 9999 whose prime factors are all less than 20; decided from the prime factorisation, with the grouping of equal primes in pairs shown; response yes or no |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Is 1156 a perfect square? Is 2400 a perfect square? |
