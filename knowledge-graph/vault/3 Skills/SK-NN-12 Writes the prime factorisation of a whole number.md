# SK-NN-12

> Writes the prime factorisation of a whole number.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M804 Finds squares, cubes and square roots and cube roots]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | writes |
| Content | a whole number as a product of primes |
| Conditions | whole numbers from 2 to 999 999 whose prime factors are all less than 20; by repeated division or a factor tree; answer a product of primes, with repeated primes written out or in index form |
| Role | step; carried out inside [[SK-NN-14 Decides whether a whole number is a perfect square from its]] (always), [[SK-NN-15 Decides whether a whole number is a perfect cube from its]] (always), [[SK-NN-16 Finds the smallest number by which a whole number must be]] (always), [[SK-NN-17 Finds the smallest number by which a whole number must be]] (always), [[SK-NN-18 Finds the square root of a perfect square by prime]] (always), [[SK-NN-23 Finds the cube root of a perfect cube by prime factorisation]] (always) |
| Example task | Write 1764 as a product of prime factors. |
