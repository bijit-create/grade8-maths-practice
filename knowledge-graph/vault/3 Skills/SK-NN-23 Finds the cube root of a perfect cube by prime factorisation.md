# SK-NN-23

> Finds the cube root of a perfect cube by prime factorisation.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M804 Finds squares, cubes and square roots and cube roots]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the cube root of a perfect cube |
| Conditions | perfect cubes from 125 to 999 999 whose prime factors are all less than 20; by prime factorisation with the primes grouped in triples; answer a whole number |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Find the cube root of 13824 by prime factorisation. |
