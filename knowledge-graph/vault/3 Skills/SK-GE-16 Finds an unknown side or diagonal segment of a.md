# SK-GE-16

> Finds an unknown side or diagonal segment of a parallelogram, rhombus or rectangle from given lengths, using its properties.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M812 Verifies properties of parallelograms and establishes]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | an unknown side or a segment of a diagonal of a parallelogram, rhombus or rectangle |
| Conditions | lengths given in whole or half centimetres from 2 cm to 12 cm; unknown sides, or the segments into which the diagonals cut each other; response: the length in centimetres with the property used stated |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | The diagonals of rectangle ABCD meet at O. AC = 12 cm. Find BD and OB, giving the property used for each. |
