# SK-NN-25

> Simplifies a numerical expression of powers of one base with integer exponents, using the laws of exponents.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M805 Solves problems with integral exponents]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | simplifies |
| Content | an expression of powers of one base multiplied, divided or raised to a power |
| Conditions | one base, a non-zero integer from -10 to 10 or a fraction; integer exponents from -10 to 10 including 0; up to four powers combined by × and ÷, with at most one power of a power; a negative base written in brackets; answer as a single power with a positive exponent, or as a number when the task says so; written symbols |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Simplify (3^(−7) ÷ 3^(−10)) × 3^(−5) and write the answer as a power with a positive exponent.   Simplify (−4)^5 ÷ (−4)^8. |
