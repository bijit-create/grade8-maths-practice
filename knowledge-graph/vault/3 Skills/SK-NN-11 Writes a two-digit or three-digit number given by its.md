# SK-NN-11

> Writes a two-digit or three-digit number given by its digits in generalised form.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M803 Proves divisibility rules of 2, 3,4, 5, 6, 9 and 11]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | writes |
| Content | a number given by its digits as 10a + b or 100a + 10b + c |
| Conditions | two-digit number with digits a and b, or three-digit number with digits a, b and c, given as ab or abc or as a numeral such as 47 or 358; response in letters or in expanded form |
| Role | step; carried out inside [[SK-NN-06 Justifies the divisibility rule of 2 or 5 for a two-digit]] (always), [[SK-NN-07 Justifies the divisibility rule of 4 for a three-digit]] (always), [[SK-NN-08 Justifies the divisibility rule of 3 or 9 for a two-digit]] (always), [[SK-NN-09 Justifies the divisibility rule of 11 for a two-digit or]] (always) |
| Example task | Write 47 as 10 × 4 + 7, and write the number ab and the number abc in generalised form. |
