# SK-NN-29

> Writes a number in standard form.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M805 Solves problems with integral exponents]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | writes |
| Content | a positive number as a × 10^n with 1 ≤ a < 10 |
| Conditions | whole numbers with 4 to 15 digits; decimals less than 1 with up to 20 decimal places; exponent an integer; answer a × 10^n with a a decimal and n an integer; written symbols |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Write 0.000000564 and 5 967 000 000 in standard form. |
