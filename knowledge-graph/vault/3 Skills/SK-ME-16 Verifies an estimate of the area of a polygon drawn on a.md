# SK-ME-16

> Verifies an estimate of the area of a polygon drawn on a square grid by finding its area with a formula and comparing the two values.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M816 Estimates the area of shapes like trapezium and other]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | verifies |
| Content | an estimate of an area, checked against the area found by a formula |
| Conditions | trapezium, quadrilateral with a diagonal along a grid line, or pentagon or hexagon that splits into triangles and trapeziums along grid lines; grid of 1 cm squares with the vertices at grid points; the lengths for the formula read off the grid by counting units; the estimate given as a number of square centimetres; response: the area by the formula, the difference from the estimate, and a statement whether the estimate is close |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | On a grid of 1 cm squares a trapezium has parallel sides of 10 cm and 6 cm, 4 cm apart. A learner counted squares and estimated its area as 33 cm². Find the area by the formula and say whether the estimate is close. |
