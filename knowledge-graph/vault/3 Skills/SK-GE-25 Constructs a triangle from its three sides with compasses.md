# SK-GE-25

> Constructs a triangle from its three sides with compasses and a straight edge.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M815 Constructs different quadrilaterals using compasses]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | constructs |
| Content | a triangle from three given side lengths |
| Conditions | side lengths from 2 cm to 8 cm to the nearest half centimetre that satisfy the triangle condition; compasses and a straight edge marked in centimetres; response: the construction with the arcs left visible |
| Role | step; carried out inside [[SK-GE-26 Constructs a quadrilateral from its four sides and one]] (always), [[SK-GE-27 Constructs a quadrilateral from its two diagonals and three]] (always) |
| Example task | Construct triangle ABC with AB = 4.5 cm, BC = 5.5 cm and AC = 7 cm. |
