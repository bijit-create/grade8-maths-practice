# SK-PR-11

> Says whether the recorded result of repeated trials of a coin, a die or a spinner is close to the result expected from the theoretical probability of the event.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M820 Makes hypotheses on chances of future events on the]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | says |
| Content | whether a recorded result agrees with the theoretical probability |
| Conditions | 40 to 200 trials of a fair coin, a six-faced die, a spinner with equal sectors, or a counter drawn at random from a bag and put back; one event of one or several outcomes; recorded count chosen to be clearly close to or clearly far from the expected count; the learner writes the two fractions or the expected and recorded counts, then a verdict with a reason in one sentence |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | A coin is tossed 100 times and lands heads 46 times. Write the theoretical probability of heads, the number of heads expected in 100 tosses, and say whether 46 is close to what was expected. |
