# SK-AG-40

> Finds the product of two numbers near the same base using the identity for (x + a)(x + b).

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M808 Uses various algebraic identities in solving problems]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the product of two numbers that differ from a common base by small amounts |
| Conditions | whole numbers or decimals with one decimal place; the base x is a multiple of 5 from 5 to 100 and each number differs from x by a whole number from 1 to 5, or by 0.1 to 0.3 for decimals; a and b may be positive or negative and are not equal in size, as in 103 × 104, 103 × 98, 5.1 × 5.2 and 9.7 × 9.8; written working; numerical answer |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Find 103 × 104 using an identity.   Find 9.7 × 9.8. |
