# SK-GE-15

> Finds an unknown angle of a parallelogram, rhombus or rectangle from given measures, using its properties.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M812 Verifies properties of parallelograms and establishes]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | an unknown angle of a parallelogram, rhombus or rectangle |
| Conditions | one or two angles given in whole degrees, or the angle between the diagonals of a rhombus; unknown angles at the vertices or where the diagonals meet; response: the angle in degrees with the property used stated |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | In parallelogram ABCD, ∠A = 65°. Find ∠B, ∠C and ∠D, giving the property used for each. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Statement | Pass: one principal verb. |
| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-GE-16 Finds an unknown side or diagonal segment of a]]. A learner gives ∠B = 115°, ∠C = 65° and ∠D = 115° for a parallelogram with ∠A = 65°, but with diagonal AC = 10 cm gives AO = 10 cm for the segment from A to the point O where the diagonals meet. |
| Stopping rule | One angle property is applied to one given angle in every task; no smaller task can be set. |
