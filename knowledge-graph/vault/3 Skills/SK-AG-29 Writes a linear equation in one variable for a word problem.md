# SK-AG-29

> Writes a linear equation in one variable for a word problem about numbers, ages, digits, money, perimeter or fractions of a quantity.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M806 Solves puzzles and daily life problems using variables]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | writes |
| Content | a linear equation in one variable for a word problem |
| Conditions | one unknown, the letter given or chosen by the learner; situations of consecutive numbers, ages now and after or before some years, digits of a two-digit number, quantities in a ratio, perimeter, money in coins or notes and fractions of a quantity; the equation may have the variable on both sides, a bracket or fractions; the equation is not solved; response as an equation |
| Role | step; carried out inside [[SK-AG-25 Solves a word problem about numbers, ages, money or]] (always), [[SK-AG-26 Solves a puzzle about the digits of a two-digit number by]] (always), [[SK-AG-27 Solves a word problem by writing and solving a linear]] (always), [[SK-AG-28 Solves a word problem by writing and solving a linear]] (always) |
| Example task | The present age of Sahil's mother is three times the present age of Sahil. After 5 years their ages will add to 66 years. Write an equation for Sahil's present age x. |
