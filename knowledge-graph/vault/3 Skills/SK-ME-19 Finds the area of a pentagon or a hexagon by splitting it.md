# SK-ME-19

> Finds the area of a pentagon or a hexagon by splitting it into triangles and trapeziums.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M817 Finds the area of a polygon]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the area of a pentagon or a hexagon as the sum of the areas of triangles and trapeziums |
| Conditions | pentagon or hexagon drawn with one diagonal and the perpendicular distances from the other vertices to it marked; two to four pieces; lengths in cm or m, whole numbers or one decimal place; response: area with square units and working that shows each piece |
| Role | outcome task; carried out inside [[SK-ME-16 Verifies an estimate of the area of a polygon drawn on a]] (sometimes) |
| Example task | In pentagon ABCDE the diagonal AD is 12 cm long. The perpendiculars from B and C to AD meet it at M and N, with AM = 3 cm, ND = 2 cm, BM = 4 cm and CN = 5 cm. The perpendicular from E to AD is 3 cm long. Find the area of ABCDE. |
