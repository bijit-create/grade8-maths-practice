# SK-GE-26

> Constructs a quadrilateral from its four sides and one diagonal with compasses and a straight edge.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M815 Constructs different quadrilaterals using compasses]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | constructs |
| Content | a quadrilateral from four sides and a diagonal |
| Conditions | lengths from 2 cm to 8 cm to the nearest half centimetre, the five lengths forming two triangles that can be constructed on the diagonal; compasses and a straight edge marked in centimetres; response: the construction with the arcs left visible |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Construct quadrilateral ABCD with AB = 4.5 cm, BC = 5.5 cm, CD = 4 cm, AD = 6 cm and AC = 7 cm. |
