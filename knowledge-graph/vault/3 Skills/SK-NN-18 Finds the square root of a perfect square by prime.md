# SK-NN-18

> Finds the square root of a perfect square by prime factorisation.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M804 Finds squares, cubes and square roots and cube roots]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the square root of a perfect square |
| Conditions | perfect squares from 100 to 9999 whose prime factors are all less than 20; by prime factorisation with the primes grouped in pairs; answer a whole number |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Find the square root of 1764 by prime factorisation. |

| Stopping rule | One procedure: factorise, group in pairs, take one prime from each pair; the factorisation is a step and the grouping cannot be practised apart from the root. |
