# SK-ME-14

> Solves a problem from a real-life situation that needs the volume or the surface area of a cuboid or a cube.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M818 Finds surface area and volume of cuboidal and]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | solves |
| Content | a situation that calls for the volume or the surface area of a cuboid or a cube, sometimes followed by a cost or a count |
| Conditions | situation in words: filling a tank or a box, packing cubes into a box, painting the walls of a room, making or wrapping a box, including a box or tank without a lid; edges in m and cm, written in one unit where needed; may end with a cost or a count; two to four steps; response: the answer with units and its meaning in the situation |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | An open water tank is 2 m long, 1.5 m wide and 1 m deep. Find the area of metal sheet needed to make it. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Statement | Pass: one principal verb. |
| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-ME-12 Finds the total surface area of a cuboid or a cube from its]]. A learner finds the total surface area of a 2 m by 1.5 m by 1 m cuboid as 13 m², but for the open tank also gives 13 m², including the top that the tank does not have. |
| Stopping rule | Deciding from the situation between volume and surface area, and which faces count, is one decision; the calculations inside it are separate step skills. |
