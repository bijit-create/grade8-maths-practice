# SK-AG-36

> Expands the product of a binomial and a trinomial.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M807 Multiplies algebraic expressions. E.g. Expands (2x-5)]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | expands |
| Content | the product of a binomial and a trinomial |
| Conditions | a binomial and a trinomial in one or two variables with powers up to 2 in the trinomial, as in (a + 7)(a² + 3a + 5) and (x + y)(x² − xy + y²); coefficients integers from −20 to 20 or fractions with denominator up to 10; written symbols; answer with like terms collected |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Expand (a + 7)(a² + 3a + 5).   Expand (x + y)(x² − xy + y²). |

| Stopping rule | All six products are formed and written together; splitting by product gives parts that cannot be evidenced apart. |
