# SK-GE-10

> Finds the angles of a quadrilateral from angles given in a ratio.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M811 Solves problems related to angles of a quadrilateral]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the angles of a quadrilateral whose angles are in a given ratio |
| Conditions | ratio of four whole numbers from 1 to 15 whose sum divides 360 exactly, each angle less than 180°; response: each angle in degrees |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | The angles of a quadrilateral are in the ratio 3 : 5 : 9 : 13. Find all the angles. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Statement | Pass: one principal verb. |
| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-GE-09 Finds the angles of a quadrilateral from angles given as]]. A learner finds 36°, 60°, 108° and 156° for the ratio 3 : 5 : 9 : 13, but for angles x°, (x + 20)°, (2x - 10)° and 90° forgets the 20, -10 and 90, writes x + x + 2x = 360 and gives x = 90. |
| Stopping rule | Sharing 360° in a ratio is one act; the sum of the ratio terms and the share of each angle are found in every task. |
