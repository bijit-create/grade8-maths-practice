# SK-AG-38

> Finds the square of a number near a multiple of ten or a hundred using the identity for (a + b)² or (a − b)².

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M808 Uses various algebraic identities in solving problems]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the square of a whole number or a decimal near a multiple of 10 or 100 |
| Conditions | whole numbers from 20 to 1000 that are 1, 2 or 3 more or less than a multiple of 10 or of 100, as 71, 99, 102 and 998; decimals with one decimal place that are 0.1 to 0.3 more or less than a whole number, as 5.2 and 8.9; the number written as a sum or a difference and squared by the identity; written working; numerical answer |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Find 103² using an identity.   Find 98².   Find 8.9². |
