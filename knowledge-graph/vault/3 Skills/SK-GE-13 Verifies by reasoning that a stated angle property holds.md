# SK-GE-13

> Verifies by reasoning that a stated angle property holds for every parallelogram, using parallel lines and a transversal.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M812 Verifies properties of parallelograms and establishes]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | verifies |
| Content | that opposite angles of a parallelogram are equal or that adjacent angles add up to 180°, by reasoning |
| Conditions | parallelogram ABCD with AB parallel to DC and AD parallel to BC drawn and not to scale; properties: adjacent angles add up to 180°, opposite angles are equal; reason from co-interior angles between parallel lines; response: a chain of statements, each with its reason |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | ABCD is a parallelogram. Show that ∠A = ∠C, giving a reason for each step. |
