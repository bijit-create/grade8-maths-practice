# SK-AG-33

> Multiplies two or more monomials.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M807 Multiplies algebraic expressions. E.g. Expands (2x-5)]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | multiplies |
| Content | monomials in one to three variables |
| Conditions | two to four monomials; each a number multiplied by powers of one to three variables, with powers up to 4, as in 5x, 4x², x²y and pq³; numerical coefficients integers from −20 to 20 or fractions with denominator up to 10, positive or negative; written symbols; answer a single monomial |
| Role | outcome task; carried out inside [[SK-AG-34 Expands the product of a monomial and a polynomial]] (always), [[SK-AG-35 Expands the product of two binomials]] (always), [[SK-AG-36 Expands the product of a binomial and a trinomial]] (always) |
| Example task | Find the product of 5x and 4x².   (2/3 xy) × (−9/10 x²y²) = ?   x × x² × x³ × x⁴ = ? |
