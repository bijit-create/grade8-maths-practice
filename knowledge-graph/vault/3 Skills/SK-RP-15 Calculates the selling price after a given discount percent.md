# SK-RP-15

> Calculates the selling price after a given discount percent on a marked price.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M809 Applies the concept of percent in profit and loss]].

|---|---|

| Conditions | marked price up to ₹50,000; discount a whole-number percent |
