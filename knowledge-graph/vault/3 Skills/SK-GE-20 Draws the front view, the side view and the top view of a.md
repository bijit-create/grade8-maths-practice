# SK-GE-20

> Draws the front view, the side view and the top view of a cuboid, a cylinder, a cone, a sphere or an object made of two of these solids.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M813 Represents 3D shapes on a plane surface such as sheet]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | draws |
| Content | the three views of a simple solid or of an object made of two simple solids |
| Conditions | cuboid, cube, cylinder, cone, sphere, or an object with one of these standing on the top face of another and within it; each solid standing on a flat face or its base; response: three freehand drawings that show the shapes and the relative positions |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Draw the front view, side view and top view of a cylinder standing on a cuboid. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|

| Stopping rule | Each view of a simple solid is one known figure or a combination of two; no smaller task shows the representation. |
