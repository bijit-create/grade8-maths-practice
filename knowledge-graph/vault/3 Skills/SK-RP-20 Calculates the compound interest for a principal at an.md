# SK-RP-20

> Calculates the compound interest for a principal at an annual rate compounded half-yearly.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M809 Applies the concept of percent in profit and loss]].

|---|---|
| Action | calculates |
| Content | compound interest compounded half-yearly |
