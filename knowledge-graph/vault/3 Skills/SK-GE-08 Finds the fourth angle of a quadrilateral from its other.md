# SK-GE-08

> Finds the fourth angle of a quadrilateral from its other three angles, each given in degrees.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M811 Solves problems related to angles of a quadrilateral]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the fourth angle of a quadrilateral, using the angle sum property |
| Conditions | convex quadrilateral given as a figure or in words; three given angles whole numbers of degrees, each less than 180°, with a sum more than 180° and less than 360°; response: the fourth angle in degrees with the property stated as the reason |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Three angles of a quadrilateral are 75°, 90° and 105°. Find the fourth angle. |
