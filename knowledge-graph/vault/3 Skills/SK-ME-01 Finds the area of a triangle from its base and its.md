# SK-ME-01

> Finds the area of a triangle from its base and its perpendicular height.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M817 Finds the area of a polygon]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the area of a triangle |
| Conditions | acute, right-angled and obtuse triangles, with the height inside or outside the triangle; slanting sides may be shown as extra lengths; lengths in cm or m, whole numbers or one decimal place; response: area with square units |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | A triangle has base 12 cm, perpendicular height 7.5 cm and slanting sides 9 cm and 10 cm. Find its area. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Statement | Pass: one principal verb. |
| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-ME-02 Finds the area of a parallelogram from its base and its]]. A learner finds the area of a parallelogram with base 12 cm and height 7.5 cm as 90 cm², but gives 90 cm² for the triangle with the same base and height, leaving out the half. |
| Stopping rule | Triangles of every shape use one formula with the height at right angles to the base; splitting by type of triangle gives tasks of one type. |
