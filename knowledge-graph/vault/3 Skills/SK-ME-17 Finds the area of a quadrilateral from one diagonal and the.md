# SK-ME-17

> Finds the area of a quadrilateral from one diagonal and the perpendiculars to it from the two opposite vertices.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M817 Finds the area of a polygon]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the area of a quadrilateral as the sum of the areas of the two triangles on its diagonal |
| Conditions | convex quadrilaterals with one diagonal and the two perpendiculars to it drawn and marked; lengths in cm or m, whole numbers or one decimal place; response: area with square units |
| Role | outcome task; carried out inside [[SK-ME-16 Verifies an estimate of the area of a polygon drawn on a]] (sometimes) |
| Example task | In quadrilateral ABCD the diagonal AC is 12 cm long. The perpendiculars from B and D to AC are 4 cm and 5 cm long. Find the area of ABCD. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Statement | Pass: one principal verb. |
| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-ME-01 Finds the area of a triangle from its base and its]]. A learner finds the area of a triangle with base 12 cm and height 4 cm as 24 cm², but gives the area of quadrilateral ABCD, with diagonal AC = 12 cm and perpendiculars 4 cm and 5 cm, as 24 cm², using one perpendicular and leaving out triangle ACD. |
| Stopping rule | Both triangles on the diagonal are found and added by one formula in every task; the area of a single triangle is the task of SK-ME-01. |
