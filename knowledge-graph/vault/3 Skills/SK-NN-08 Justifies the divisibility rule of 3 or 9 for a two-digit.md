# SK-NN-08

> Justifies the divisibility rule of 3 or 9 for a two-digit or three-digit number from its generalised form.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M803 Proves divisibility rules of 2, 3,4, 5, 6, 9 and 11]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | justifies |
| Content | why a number is divisible by 3 or by 9 exactly when the sum of its digits is |
| Conditions | two-digit number ab or three-digit number abc given by its digits, not in generalised form; rule stated in the task (the sum of the digits decides); response in words or in letters |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Show why a three-digit number abc is divisible by 9 exactly when a + b + c is divisible by 9. |

| Stopping rule | One justification: abc = 99a + 9b + (a + b + c), and the divisors 3 and 9 differ only in that 99a + 9b is a multiple of both. |
