# SK-GE-11

> Finds an interior angle of a quadrilateral from the angle that makes a straight line with it at the same vertex.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M811 Solves problems related to angles of a quadrilateral]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | an interior angle of a quadrilateral from the exterior angle at the same vertex |
| Conditions | figure of a convex quadrilateral with one side extended beyond a vertex and the angle between the extension and the next side marked in whole degrees from 30° to 150°; response: the interior angle in degrees with the reason |
| Role | step; carried out inside [[SK-GE-08 Finds the fourth angle of a quadrilateral from its other]] (sometimes) |
| Example task | One side of a quadrilateral is extended beyond vertex C. The angle between the extension and side CD is 70°. Find the interior angle at C. |
