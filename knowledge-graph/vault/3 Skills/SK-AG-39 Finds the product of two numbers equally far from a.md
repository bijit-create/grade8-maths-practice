# SK-AG-39

> Finds the product of two numbers equally far from a multiple of ten on either side using the identity for (a + b)(a − b).

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M808 Uses various algebraic identities in solving problems]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the product of two whole numbers a − b and a + b |
| Conditions | two whole numbers a − b and a + b, where a is a multiple of 10 from 50 to 300 and b is 1, 2 or 3, as 98 × 102, 51 × 49, 78 × 82 and 297 × 303; written working; numerical answer |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Find 98 × 102 using an identity.   Find 51 × 49.   Find 297 × 303. |

| Stopping rule | Seeing the pair as a − b and a + b and writing the difference of squares are done in one pass; splitting them gives parts that cannot be evidenced apart. |
