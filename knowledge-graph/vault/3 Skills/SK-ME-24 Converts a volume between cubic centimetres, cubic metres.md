# SK-ME-24

> Converts a volume between cubic centimetres, cubic metres and litres.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M818 Finds surface area and volume of cuboidal and]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | converts |
| Content | a volume or a capacity written in cm³, m³ or litres |
| Conditions | cm³ to litres and litres to cm³, m³ to cm³, and m³ to litres and litres to m³; amounts that are whole numbers or have one decimal place; one conversion in each task; response: the amount in the unit asked for |
| Role | step; carried out inside [[SK-ME-25 Solves a problem from a real-life situation that needs the]] (sometimes) |
| Example task | Write 2.5 m³ in litres. |
