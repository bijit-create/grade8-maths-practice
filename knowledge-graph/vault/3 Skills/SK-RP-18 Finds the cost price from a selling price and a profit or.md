# SK-RP-18

> Finds the cost price from a selling price and a profit or loss percent.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M809 Applies the concept of percent in profit and loss]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the cost price |
| Conditions | whole-rupee selling price up to ₹50,000; whole-number percent |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | A watch is sold for ₹1,380 at a profit of 15%. Find the cost price. |
