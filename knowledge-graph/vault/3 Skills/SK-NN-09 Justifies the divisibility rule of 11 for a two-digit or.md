# SK-NN-09

> Justifies the divisibility rule of 11 for a two-digit or three-digit number from its generalised form.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M803 Proves divisibility rules of 2, 3,4, 5, 6, 9 and 11]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | justifies |
| Content | why a number is divisible by 11 exactly when the difference of the sums of its digits in odd and even places is 0 or divisible by 11 |
| Conditions | two-digit number ab or three-digit number abc given by its digits, not in generalised form; rule stated in the task; response in words or in letters; a remainder expression such as a + c − b may be negative |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Show why a three-digit number abc is divisible by 11 exactly when (a + c) − b is 0 or divisible by 11, and why a two-digit number ab is divisible by 11 exactly when a = b. |
