# SK-ME-21

> Finds the volume of a cylinder from its radius or its diameter and its height.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M818 Finds surface area and volume of cuboidal and]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the volume of a cylinder |
| Conditions | radius or diameter and height given in the same unit, cm or m, whole numbers or one decimal place; π given as 22/7 or 3.14 in the task; response: volume with cubic units (cm³ or m³) |
| Role | outcome task; carried out inside [[SK-ME-25 Solves a problem from a real-life situation that needs the]] (sometimes) |
| Example task | A cylindrical drum has diameter 14 cm and height 10 cm. Find its volume. Use π = 22/7. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Statement | Pass: one principal verb. |
| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-ME-22 Finds the curved surface area of a cylinder from its radius]]. A learner finds the curved surface area of a cylinder with radius 7 cm and height 10 cm as 440 cm², but gives its volume as 22/7 × 7 × 10 = 220 cm³, using r in place of r². |
| Stopping rule | Starting from the radius or from the diameter differs by one halving inside the same task type. |
