# SK-AG-34

> Expands the product of a monomial and a polynomial.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M807 Multiplies algebraic expressions. E.g. Expands (2x-5)]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | expands |
| Content | a monomial times a polynomial of two to four terms |
| Conditions | monomial with a coefficient and up to two variables, such as 3x, −4ab and 2x²y; polynomial of two to four terms in one to three variables with powers up to 3; coefficients integers from −20 to 20 or fractions with denominator up to 10; a single product, not a sum of products; written symbols; answer written as a sum of terms |
| Role | outcome task; carried out inside [[SK-AG-35 Expands the product of two binomials]] (sometimes), [[SK-AG-36 Expands the product of a binomial and a trinomial]] (sometimes) |
| Example task | Expand 2x(3x + 5xy).   Expand −4ab(a² − 3b + 5). |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|

| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-AG-33 Multiplies two or more monomials]]. A learner finds 5x × 4x² = 20x³, but expands 3x(2x − 5) as 6x² − 5, multiplying the first term only. |
| Stopping rule | Multiplying the monomial by each term and writing the sum is one procedure; the products of single terms are the neighbouring skill. |
