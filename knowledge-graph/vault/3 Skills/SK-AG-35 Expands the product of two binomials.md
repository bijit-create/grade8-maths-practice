# SK-AG-35

> Expands the product of two binomials.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M807 Multiplies algebraic expressions. E.g. Expands (2x-5)]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | expands |
| Content | the product of two binomials |
| Conditions | each binomial has two terms in one or two variables, each term a number, a variable or a product with powers up to 2, as in (2x − 5)(3x + 7), (a + 3b)(x + 5) and (p² − q²)(2p + q); coefficients integers from −20 to 20 or fractions with denominator up to 10; written symbols; answer with like terms collected |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Expand (2x − 5)(3x + 7).   Expand (a + 3b)(x + 5). |

| Stopping rule | All four products are formed and written together; splitting by product gives parts that cannot be evidenced apart, and the collecting of like terms is recorded as a step. |
