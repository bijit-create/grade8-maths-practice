# SK-GE-29

> Constructs a quadrilateral from three sides and the two angles between them with compasses and a straight edge.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M815 Constructs different quadrilaterals using compasses]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | constructs |
| Content | a quadrilateral from three sides and the two included angles |
| Conditions | sides from 3 cm to 8 cm to the nearest half centimetre; the two angles whole numbers of degrees from 45° to 135°; compasses and a straight edge marked in centimetres, with a protractor used only to set the angles; response: the construction |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Construct quadrilateral ABCD with AB = 4 cm, BC = 5 cm, CD = 6.5 cm, ∠B = 105° and ∠C = 80°. |
