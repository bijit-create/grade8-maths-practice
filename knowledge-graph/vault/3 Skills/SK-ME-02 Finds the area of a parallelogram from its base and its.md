# SK-ME-02

> Finds the area of a parallelogram from its base and its perpendicular height.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M817 Finds the area of a polygon]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the area of a parallelogram |
| Conditions | parallelograms drawn in different orientations, with the slanting side also given; lengths in cm or m, whole numbers or one decimal place; response: area with square units |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | A parallelogram has base 9 cm, slanting side 5 cm and perpendicular height 4 cm. Find its area. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Stopping rule | One formula covers every parallelogram, rectangles included; splitting by orientation gives tasks of one type. |
