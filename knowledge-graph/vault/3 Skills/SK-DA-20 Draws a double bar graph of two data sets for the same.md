# SK-DA-20

> Draws a double bar graph of two data sets for the same categories from a table, with a key and a scale the learner chooses.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M819 Draws and interprets bar charts and pie charts]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | draws |
| Content | a double bar graph of two data sets |
| Conditions | table of 3 to 6 categories with two whole-number values each, from 5 to 500; contexts such as boys and girls, marks in two terms, rainfall in two years; graph paper; one scale for both data sets; two shades and a key; both axes labelled and the graph titled |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | The table gives the marks of one student in the half-yearly and the annual examination: Maths 60 and 75, Science 55 and 68, English 70 and 64, Hindi 62 and 80. Draw a double bar graph with a key. |
