# SK-GE-19

> Draws the front view, the side view and the top view of a solid made of cubes.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M813 Represents 3D shapes on a plane surface such as sheet]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | draws |
| Content | the three views of a solid made of unit cubes |
| Conditions | solid of 4 to 10 unit cubes, at most 3 cubes high, 3 wide and 3 deep, shown in a picture or as a model with the front marked; views drawn on a square grid with one square for each cube face seen; response: three drawings |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | A solid is made of 5 cubes: in the front row three stacks of 2, 1 and 1 cubes from left to right, and one cube behind the left stack on the ground. Draw its front view, side view and top view. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|

| Stopping rule | The three views come from the same solid read in three directions; each view is drawn from the same two facts about stacks, and no smaller task shows the representation. |
