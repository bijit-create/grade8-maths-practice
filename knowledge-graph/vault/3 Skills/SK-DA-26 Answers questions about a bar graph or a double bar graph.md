# SK-DA-26

> Answers questions about a bar graph or a double bar graph by reading the values of bars and comparing categories.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M819 Draws and interprets bar charts and pie charts]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | answers |
| Content | questions about the values and categories of a bar graph or double bar graph |
| Conditions | graph of 4 to 8 categories on a scale marked in 1s, 2s, 5s, 10s, 20s, 50s or 100s, with values on marked divisions or halfway between them; questions ask for the value of a category, the greatest or least, the difference between two categories, or, in a double bar graph, the category with the greater value in one data set or the greatest difference between the two; contexts such as sales, marks, rainfall, population; written answers with units |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | A bar graph with 1 division = 10 bicycles shows bicycles sold in six months: January 40, February 55, March 70, April 45, May 60, June 85. In which month were the most bicycles sold? How many more were sold in March than in January? |
