# SK-GE-12

> Verifies by measuring that a stated property holds for a drawn parallelogram, rhombus, rectangle or square.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M812 Verifies properties of parallelograms and establishes]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | verifies |
| Content | a property of a parallelogram or of a special parallelogram, by measuring one drawn figure |
| Conditions | figure drawn on paper with sides from 3 cm to 8 cm; ruler read to the nearest millimetre and protractor to the nearest degree; properties: opposite sides equal, opposite angles equal, adjacent angles add up to 180°, diagonals bisect each other, diagonals of a rhombus meet at right angles, diagonals of a rectangle are equal; response: the measures recorded and a statement of whether the property holds within measuring error |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Measure the sides and angles of the drawn parallelogram ABCD. Does it have opposite angles equal? |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Statement | Pass: one principal verb. |
| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-GE-13 Verifies by reasoning that a stated angle property holds]]. A learner measures ∠A = 62° and ∠C = 62° in a drawn parallelogram and writes that opposite angles are equal, but asked why this must be so in every parallelogram writes 'because I measured them'. |
| Stopping rule | Measuring the figure and comparing the measures with the stated property are done together in every task; no smaller task shows a verification. |
