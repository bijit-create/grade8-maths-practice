# SK-GE-09

> Finds the angles of a quadrilateral from angles given as expressions in x.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M811 Solves problems related to angles of a quadrilateral]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the angles of a quadrilateral whose angles are given as expressions in one variable |
| Conditions | four angles each a whole number of degrees or an expression ax + b with a from 1 to 4 and b from -20 to 40, at least two containing x; the equation has a whole-number solution; response: the value of x and each angle in degrees |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | The angles of a quadrilateral are x°, (x + 20)°, (2x - 10)° and 90°. Find x and each angle. |
