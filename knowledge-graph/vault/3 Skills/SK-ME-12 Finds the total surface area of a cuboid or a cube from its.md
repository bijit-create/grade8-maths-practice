# SK-ME-12

> Finds the total surface area of a cuboid or a cube from its edge lengths.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M818 Finds surface area and volume of cuboidal and]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the total surface area of a cuboid or a cube |
| Conditions | length, width and height given in the same unit, cm or m, whole numbers or one decimal place; a cube given by one edge; all six faces counted; response: area with square units (cm² or m²) |
| Role | outcome task; carried out inside [[SK-ME-14 Solves a problem from a real-life situation that needs the]] (sometimes) |
| Example task | Find the total surface area of a box 12 cm long, 8 cm wide and 5 cm high. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Statement | Pass: one principal verb. |
| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-ME-11 Finds the volume of a cuboid or a cube from its edge lengths]]. A learner finds the volume of a cube with edge 5 cm as 125 cm³, but gives its surface area as 4 × 25 = 100 cm², counting only the four side faces. |
| Stopping rule | The six faces of a cuboid are three pairs of equal rectangles in every task; boxes without a lid are a condition of the problem skill SK-ME-14. |
