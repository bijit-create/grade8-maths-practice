# SK-GE-27

> Constructs a quadrilateral from its two diagonals and three sides with compasses and a straight edge.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M815 Constructs different quadrilaterals using compasses]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | constructs |
| Content | a quadrilateral from two diagonals and three sides |
| Conditions | lengths from 2 cm to 10 cm to the nearest half centimetre, the five lengths forming a first triangle from two sides and a diagonal and a fourth vertex that the remaining side and diagonal reach; compasses and a straight edge marked in centimetres; response: the construction with the arcs left visible |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Construct quadrilateral LIFT with LI = 4 cm, IF = 3 cm, TL = 2.5 cm, LF = 4.5 cm and IT = 4 cm. |
