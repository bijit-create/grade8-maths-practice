# SK-PR-08

> Calculates the experimental probability of an event from the recorded results of trials.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M820 Makes hypotheses on chances of future events on the]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | calculates |
| Content | the experimental probability of an event |
| Conditions | 20 to 200 trials, from a simulation the learner has run and recorded in a tally or frequency table, or results given in a table; answer as a fraction, a decimal or a percentage as asked |
| Role | step; carried out inside [[SK-PR-09 Writes, as a fraction, the chance that an event happens in]] (always), [[SK-PR-11 Says whether the recorded result of repeated trials of a]] (always) |
| Example task | A spinner with three equal sectors was spun 50 times: red 18, blue 21, yellow 11. Find the experimental probability of landing on blue, as a decimal and as a percentage. |
