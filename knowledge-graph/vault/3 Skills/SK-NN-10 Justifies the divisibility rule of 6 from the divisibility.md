# SK-NN-10

> Justifies the divisibility rule of 6 from the divisibility of a number by 2 and by 3.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M803 Proves divisibility rules of 2, 3,4, 5, 6, 9 and 11]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | justifies |
| Content | why a number is divisible by 6 exactly when it is divisible by both 2 and 3 |
| Conditions | whole numbers of two or three digits used to show the reason, as in 156; rule stated in the task; response in words or in letters |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Explain why a number divisible by 6, such as 156, is divisible by both 2 and 3, and why a number divisible by both 2 and 3 is divisible by 6. |
