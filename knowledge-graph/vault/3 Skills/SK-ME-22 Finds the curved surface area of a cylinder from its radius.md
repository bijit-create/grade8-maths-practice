# SK-ME-22

> Finds the curved surface area of a cylinder from its radius or its diameter and its height.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M818 Finds surface area and volume of cuboidal and]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the area of the curved surface of a cylinder |
| Conditions | radius or diameter and height given in the same unit, cm or m, whole numbers or one decimal place; π given as 22/7 or 3.14 in the task; the two circular ends not counted; response: area with square units (cm² or m²) |
| Role | outcome task; carried out inside [[SK-ME-25 Solves a problem from a real-life situation that needs the]] (sometimes) |
| Example task | A cylindrical tin has radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. Find the area of its curved surface, where the label goes. Use π = 22/7. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Statement | Pass: one principal verb. |
| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-ME-21 Finds the volume of a cylinder from its radius or its]]. A learner finds the volume of a cylinder with radius 7 cm and height 10 cm as 1540 cm³, but gives its curved surface area as 22/7 × 7 × 10 = 220 cm², leaving out the factor 2. |
| Stopping rule | Starting from the radius or from the diameter differs by one halving inside the same task type. |
