# SK-RN-40

> Gives a counterexample that shows a stated property does not hold for a stated operation on rational numbers.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M801 Generalises properties of addition, subtraction,]].

|---|---|
| Action | gives |
| Content | a counterexample |
