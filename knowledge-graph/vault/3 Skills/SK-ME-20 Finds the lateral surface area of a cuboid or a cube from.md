# SK-ME-20

> Finds the lateral surface area of a cuboid or a cube from its edge lengths.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M818 Finds surface area and volume of cuboidal and]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the area of the four side faces of a cuboid or a cube |
| Conditions | length, width and height given in the same unit, cm or m, whole numbers or one decimal place; a cube given by one edge; the top and the bottom not counted; response: area with square units (cm² or m²) |
| Role | outcome task; carried out inside [[SK-ME-25 Solves a problem from a real-life situation that needs the]] (sometimes) |
| Example task | Find the area of the four walls of a hall 10 m long, 8 m wide and 4 m high. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Statement | Pass: one principal verb. |
| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-ME-12 Finds the total surface area of a cuboid or a cube from its]]. A learner finds the total surface area of a box 12 cm long, 8 cm wide and 5 cm high as 392 cm², but gives its lateral surface area as 392 cm², counting the top and the bottom as well. |
| Stopping rule | The four side faces are two pairs of equal rectangles in every task, and a cube is a cuboid with equal edges, so cubes and cuboids give one task type. |
