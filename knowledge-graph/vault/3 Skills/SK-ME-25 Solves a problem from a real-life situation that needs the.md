# SK-ME-25

> Solves a problem from a real-life situation that needs the volume, the capacity or the surface area of a cuboid, a cube or a cylinder.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M818 Finds surface area and volume of cuboidal and]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | solves |
| Content | a situation that calls for a volume, a capacity or a surface area of a cuboid, a cube or a cylinder, sometimes followed by a cost or a count |
| Conditions | situation in words, with a diagram where useful: filling a tank, a drum or a cistern and giving its capacity in litres, packing, painting the four walls of a room or the curved surface of a pillar, wrapping a box, making a tin, a garden roller, including a tank or a box without a lid; dimensions in m and cm, written in one unit where needed; π given as 22/7 or 3.14; may need a conversion of volume to litres or between m³ and cm³; may end with a cost or a count; two to four steps; response: the answer with units and its meaning in the situation |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | A cylindrical tank has radius 0.7 m and height 2 m. How many litres of water does it hold when full? Use π = 22/7. |
