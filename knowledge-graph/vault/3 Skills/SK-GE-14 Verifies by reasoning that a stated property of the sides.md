# SK-GE-14

> Verifies by reasoning that a stated property of the sides or the diagonals holds for every parallelogram, using congruent triangles.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M812 Verifies properties of parallelograms and establishes]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | verifies |
| Content | that opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal or that its diagonals bisect each other, by reasoning |
| Conditions | parallelogram ABCD with AB parallel to DC and AD parallel to BC drawn and not to scale; properties: opposite sides are equal, diagonals bisect each other; reasons: alternate angles between parallel lines, ASA congruence of two triangles formed by a diagonal, equal matching sides of congruent triangles; response: a chain of statements, each with its reason |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | ABCD is a parallelogram. Draw the diagonal AC and show that AB = DC, giving a reason for each step. |
