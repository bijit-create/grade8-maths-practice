# SK-DA-21

> Chooses a scale for the axis of a bar graph so that the greatest value fits on the axis and the divisions are equal.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M819 Draws and interprets bar charts and pie charts]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | chooses |
| Content | a scale for the value axis of a bar graph |
| Conditions | 4 to 8 whole-number values from 5 to 500; axis of 10 to 20 cm or 10 to 20 squares of graph paper; scale of 1 division for 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50 or 100 units; written as, for example, 1 cm = 10 students |
| Role | step; carried out inside [[SK-DA-19 Draws a bar graph of a data set from a table, with a scale]] (always), [[SK-DA-20 Draws a double bar graph of two data sets for the same]] (always) |
| Example task | The values are 45, 30, 15, 20 and 10 and the axis is 10 cm long. Write a scale and say how long the bar for 45 is. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Statement | Pass: one principal verb. |
| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-DA-19 Draws a bar graph of a data set from a table, with a scale]]. A learner writes the scale 1 cm = 5 students for the values 45, 30, 15, 20 and 10 but draws the bars 4.5 cm, 3 cm, 1.5 cm, 2 cm and 1 cm long, dividing each value by 10 instead of by 5. |
| Stopping rule | Choosing the scale and stating the bar length it gives for one value can be practised apart from drawing the whole graph; a smaller part is only dividing a value by the scale, which gives no new task type. |
