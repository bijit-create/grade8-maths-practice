# SK-NN-07

> Justifies the divisibility rule of 4 for a three-digit number from its generalised form.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M803 Proves divisibility rules of 2, 3,4, 5, 6, 9 and 11]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | justifies |
| Content | why a three-digit number is divisible by 4 exactly when the number formed by its last two digits is |
| Conditions | three-digit number abc given by its digits, not in generalised form; rule stated in the task (the last two digits decide); response in words or in letters |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Show why a three-digit number abc is divisible by 4 exactly when the number 10b + c formed by its last two digits is divisible by 4 (for example 316, because 16 is divisible by 4). |
