# SK-GE-23

> Counts the faces, vertices and edges of a prism or a pyramid.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M814 Verifies Euler's relation through pattern]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | counts |
| Content | the faces, vertices and edges of a prism or a pyramid |
| Conditions | prism or pyramid with a base of 3 to 6 sides, including the cube and the cuboid as prisms, shown as a picture or a model; response: the three numbers |
| Role | step; carried out inside [[SK-GE-24 Verifies Euler's relation, F + V E = 2, for prisms and]] (always) |
| Example task | Count the faces, vertices and edges of a pentagonal prism. |
