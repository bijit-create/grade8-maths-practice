# SK-PR-09

> Writes, as a fraction, the chance that an event happens in a further trial, using the recorded results of earlier trials of the same experiment.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M820 Makes hypotheses on chances of future events on the]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | writes |
| Content | the chance of an event in a further trial, from earlier results |
| Conditions | records of 20 to 200 trials or observations in a tally or frequency table or a list: tosses of a coin, throws of a die, spins of a spinner, or daily-life records such as days of rain in a month or matches won; events of one outcome or of several outcomes (for example 'an even number'); fraction in simplest form, written as a sentence about the next trial |
| Role | outcome task; carried out inside [[SK-PR-10 Predicts how many times an event will happen in a stated]] (always) |
| Example task | A die was thrown 60 times and a 4 came up 12 times. Write the chance, as a fraction in simplest form, that a 4 comes up on the next throw. |

| Stopping rule | Finding the fraction from the record (SK-PR-08) and stating it as the chance of the next trial are done together; the part that cannot be practised apart from the record is the statement about the next trial. |
