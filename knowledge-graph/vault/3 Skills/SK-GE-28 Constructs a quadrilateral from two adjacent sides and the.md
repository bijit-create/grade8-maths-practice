# SK-GE-28

> Constructs a quadrilateral from two adjacent sides and the angles at the three vertices of those sides with compasses and a straight edge.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M815 Constructs different quadrilaterals using compasses]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | constructs |
| Content | a quadrilateral from two adjacent sides and three angles |
| Conditions | sides from 3 cm to 8 cm to the nearest half centimetre; the three angles whole numbers of degrees from 45° to 135° with the fourth angle less than 180°; compasses and a straight edge marked in centimetres, with a protractor used only to set the angles; response: the construction |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Construct quadrilateral MORE with MO = 6 cm, OR = 4.5 cm, ∠M = 60°, ∠O = 105° and ∠R = 105°. |
