# SK-DA-24

> Writes the share of the whole shown by a sector of a pie chart as a fraction or a percentage.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M819 Draws and interprets bar charts and pie charts]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | writes |
| Content | the share of the whole shown by one sector of a pie chart |
| Conditions | pie chart of 3 to 6 sectors with the central angle of each sector given in degrees (multiples of 5), or the share given as a percentage; fraction in simplest form with denominator up to 36, or percentage up to one decimal place; written answer |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | In a pie chart of Priya's day, the sector for school has a central angle of 90°. What fraction of the day is this, and what percentage? |

| Stopping rule | Writing the angle over 360, simplifying and, when asked, converting to a percentage are done together in every task. |
