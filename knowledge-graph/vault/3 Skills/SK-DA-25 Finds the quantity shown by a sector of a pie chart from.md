# SK-DA-25

> Finds the quantity shown by a sector of a pie chart from the total of the data.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M819 Draws and interprets bar charts and pie charts]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the quantity shown by one sector of a pie chart |
| Conditions | pie chart of 3 to 6 sectors with the central angle of the sector given in degrees (multiples of 5), or its share given as a percentage; total from 100 to 1000 (marks, rupees, students, people) chosen so that the quantity is a whole number; written working and answer with its unit |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | A pie chart shows how a student's 540 marks are divided over five subjects. The sector for Mathematics has a central angle of 80°. How many marks are shown for Mathematics? |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Statement | Pass: one principal verb. |
| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-DA-24 Writes the share of the whole shown by a sector of a pie]]. A learner finds 540 × 80/360 = 120 marks for the 80° sector but, asked what fraction of the total the sector shows, writes 80/100 = 4/5, reading degrees as a percentage. |
| Stopping rule | Taking the sector's share of the total is one calculation; the share and the multiplication are done together in every task. |
