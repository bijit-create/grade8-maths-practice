# SK-NN-20

> Finds the square root of a perfect square by the division method.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M804 Finds squares, cubes and square roots and cube roots]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the square root of a perfect square |
| Conditions | perfect squares from 100 to 9999; whole numbers with an even or odd number of digits; by the division method with the digits grouped in pairs from the right; answer a whole number |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Find the square root of 4489 by the division method. |
