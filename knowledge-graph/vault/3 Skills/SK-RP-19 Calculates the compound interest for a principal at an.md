# SK-RP-19

> Calculates the compound interest for a principal at an annual rate compounded annually for up to three years.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M809 Applies the concept of percent in profit and loss]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | calculates |
| Content | compound interest compounded annually |
| Conditions | principal up to ₹1,00,000; rate a whole-number percent; one to three years; by formula or year by year |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Find the compound interest on ₹10,000 at 8% per year for 2 years, compounded annually. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Statement | Pass: one principal verb. |
| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-RP-20 Calculates the compound interest for a principal at an]]. A learner finds the compound interest on ₹10,000 at 8% per year for 2 years as ₹1,664 but, for ₹10,000 at 10% per year for 1 year compounded half-yearly, gives ₹2,100, using 10% for each half-year. |
| Stopping rule | Annual compounding is one procedure; half-yearly is a separate skill. |
