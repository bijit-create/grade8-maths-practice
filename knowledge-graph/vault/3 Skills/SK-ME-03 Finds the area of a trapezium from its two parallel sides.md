# SK-ME-03

> Finds the area of a trapezium from its two parallel sides and the perpendicular distance between them.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M817 Finds the area of a polygon]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the area of a trapezium |
| Conditions | trapeziums including right-angled and isosceles ones, parallel sides drawn horizontal or vertical; slanting sides may be shown as extra lengths; lengths in cm or m, whole numbers or one decimal place; response: area with square units |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | A trapezium-shaped plot has parallel sides 8 m and 14 m, which are 5 m apart. Find its area. |
