# SK-AG-31

> Solves a linear equation in one variable that has fractions with whole-number denominators.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M806 Solves puzzles and daily life problems using variables]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | solves |
| Content | a linear equation whose terms are fractions with whole-number denominators |
| Conditions | forms such as x/2 − 1/5 = x/3 + 1/4, (3t − 2)/4 − (2t + 3)/3 = 2/3 − t and m − (m − 1)/2 = 1 − (m − 2)/3; denominators from 2 to 12 with a common multiple up to 60; a numerator may be a linear expression, the fraction line acting as a bracket; solution an integer or a fraction with denominator up to 12, possibly negative; written working |
| Role | step; carried out inside [[SK-AG-28 Solves a word problem by writing and solving a linear]] (sometimes) |
| Example task | Solve x/2 − 1/5 = x/3 + 1/4.   Solve m − (m − 1)/2 = 1 − (m − 2)/3. |
