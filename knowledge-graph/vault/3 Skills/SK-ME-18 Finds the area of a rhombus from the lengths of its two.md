# SK-ME-18

> Finds the area of a rhombus from the lengths of its two diagonals.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M817 Finds the area of a polygon]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the area of a rhombus |
| Conditions | both diagonals given in cm or m, whole numbers or one decimal place, in a drawing or in words; response: area with square units |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | A tile is a rhombus with diagonals 12 cm and 9 cm long. Find the area of the tile. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Statement | Pass: one principal verb. |
| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-ME-17 Finds the area of a quadrilateral from one diagonal and the]]. A learner finds the area of quadrilateral ABCD, with diagonal 12 cm and perpendiculars 4 cm and 5 cm, as 54 cm², but gives the area of a rhombus with diagonals 12 cm and 9 cm as 12 × 9 = 108 cm², leaving out the half. |
| Stopping rule | One formula uses the two diagonals; no smaller part can be practised apart. |
