# SK-NN-24

> Evaluates a power with a negative integer exponent as a number.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M805 Solves problems with integral exponents]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | evaluates |
| Content | a power a^(−n) with n a positive integer |
| Conditions | integer base from -10 to 10 other than 0 with exponent from -1 to -5, a negative base written in brackets; base 10 with exponent from -1 to -10; fraction base with numerator and denominator from 1 to 10 and exponent from -1 to -3; answer an integer, a fraction, or a decimal for a power of 10; written symbols |
| Role | step; carried out inside [[SK-NN-27 Evaluates a numerical expression in which powers with]] (always), [[SK-NN-25 Simplifies a numerical expression of powers of one base]] (sometimes), [[SK-NN-26 Simplifies a numerical expression of powers with different]] (sometimes), [[SK-NN-30 Writes a number given in standard form in usual form]] (sometimes) |
| Example task | 3^(−2) = ?   (−4)^(−2) = ?   (2/3)^(−2) = ?   10^(−4) = ? |
