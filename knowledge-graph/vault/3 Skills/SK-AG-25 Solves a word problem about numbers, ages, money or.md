# SK-AG-25

> Solves a word problem about numbers, ages, money or perimeter by writing and solving a linear equation in one variable.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M806 Solves puzzles and daily life problems using variables]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | solves |
| Content | a word problem whose equation has the variable on one side only |
| Conditions | one unknown; situations of consecutive numbers or consecutive multiples, a number compared with a multiple or a fraction of itself, present ages and ages after some years, quantities in a ratio, the perimeter of a rectangle or triangle, and money in coins or notes; numbers in the situation are whole numbers, or fractions or decimals with denominator up to 10; the equation, after any bracket is expanded, has the variable terms on one side only and needs up to four steps; the equation is not given; answer stated as the quantity asked for, with its unit |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | The perimeter of a rectangle is 13 cm and its width is 2¾ cm. Find its length.   The sum of three consecutive multiples of 8 is 888. Find the multiples. |
