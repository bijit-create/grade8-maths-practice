# SK-ME-23

> Finds the total surface area of a closed cylinder from its radius or its diameter and its height.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M818 Finds surface area and volume of cuboidal and]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the total surface area of a cylinder, the curved surface and the two circular ends |
| Conditions | radius or diameter and height given in the same unit, cm or m, whole numbers or one decimal place; π given as 22/7 or 3.14 in the task; both circular ends counted; response: area with square units (cm² or m²) |
| Role | outcome task; carried out inside [[SK-ME-25 Solves a problem from a real-life situation that needs the]] (sometimes) |
| Example task | Find the total surface area of a closed cylindrical tin with radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. Use π = 22/7. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Statement | Pass: one principal verb. |
| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-ME-22 Finds the curved surface area of a cylinder from its radius]]. A learner finds the curved surface area of the tin as 440 cm², but gives its total surface area as 440 + 154 = 594 cm², adding the area of one circular end and leaving out the other. |
| Stopping rule | The curved surface and the two ends are found and added by one formula in every task; the curved surface alone is the task of SK-ME-22. |
