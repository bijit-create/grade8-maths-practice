# SK-AG-27

> Solves a word problem by writing and solving a linear equation in one variable that has the variable on both sides.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M806 Solves puzzles and daily life problems using variables]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | solves |
| Content | a word problem whose equation has the variable on both sides |
| Conditions | one unknown; two expressions in the unknown for the same quantity, as in ages at two different times (years ago, years later), quantities in a ratio with a stated difference, or a number compared with an expression in itself; whole numbers or fractions with denominator up to 10; the equation is not given and, after any bracket is expanded, has variable terms on both sides, in the forms ax + b = cx + d or a(x + b) = c(x + d) with integer coefficients from −20 to 20; answer stated as the quantity asked for, with its unit |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Arjun is twice as old as Shriya. Five years ago his age was three times Shriya's age. Find their present ages.   The number of boys and girls in a class are in the ratio 7 : 5. The number of boys is 8 more than the number of girls. What is the total class strength? |
