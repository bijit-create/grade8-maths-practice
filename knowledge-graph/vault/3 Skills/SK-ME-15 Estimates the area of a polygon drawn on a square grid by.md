# SK-ME-15

> Estimates the area of a polygon drawn on a square grid by counting the full squares and the part squares it covers.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M816 Estimates the area of shapes like trapezium and other]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | estimates |
| Content | the area of a polygon drawn on a square grid, from the squares it covers |
| Conditions | trapezium, quadrilateral, pentagon or hexagon with its vertices at grid points, drawn on a grid of 1 cm squares; the area is up to 50 cm²; squares covered by more than half, by half and by less than half all occur; response: the estimate in square centimetres |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | A trapezium is drawn on a grid of 1 cm squares. Its parallel sides, 10 cm and 6 cm long, lie along grid lines 4 cm apart, and each slanting side runs 2 cm across. Count the squares it covers and write an estimate of its area. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Statement | Pass: one principal verb. |
| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-ME-16 Verifies an estimate of the area of a polygon drawn on a]]. A learner finds the area of this trapezium as 32 cm² by the formula, but counts only the 28 full squares on the grid and gives 28 cm² as the estimate, leaving out the part squares. |
| Stopping rule | Counting the full squares and judging the part squares are done together in every task; the full squares alone do not give the estimate. |
