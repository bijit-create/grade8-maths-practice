# SK-PR-05

> Calculates the theoretical probability of an event in an experiment of one stage with equally likely outcomes.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M820 Makes hypotheses on chances of future events on the]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | calculates |
| Content | the theoretical probability of an event, one stage |
| Conditions | a fair coin, a fair die with 4, 6, 8 or 12 faces, a spinner with up to 10 equal sectors, or a counter, card or letter taken at random from up to 52 items; events described in words, including 'not' events; answer as a fraction in simplest form, or as a decimal or percentage when asked |
| Role | step; carried out inside [[SK-PR-11 Says whether the recorded result of repeated trials of a]] (always) |
| Example task | A bag holds 5 red, 3 blue and 4 green counters. One counter is taken at random. Find the probability that it is (a) blue, (b) not red. |
