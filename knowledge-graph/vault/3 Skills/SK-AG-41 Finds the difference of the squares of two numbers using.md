# SK-AG-41

> Finds the difference of the squares of two numbers using the identity for a² − b².

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M808 Uses various algebraic identities in solving problems]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the difference of two squares of numbers whose sum or difference is easy to use |
| Conditions | two whole numbers from 40 to 200, or two decimals with up to two decimal places, whose sum is 2 or a multiple of 10, as in 51² − 49², 153² − 147², (1.02)² − (0.98)² and 12.1² − 7.9²; written working; numerical answer |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Find 51² − 49² using an identity.   Find 153² − 147².   Find (1.02)² − (0.98)². |
