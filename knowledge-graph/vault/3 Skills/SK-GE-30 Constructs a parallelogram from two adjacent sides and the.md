# SK-GE-30

> Constructs a parallelogram from two adjacent sides and the angle between them with compasses and a straight edge.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M815 Constructs different quadrilaterals using compasses]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | constructs |
| Content | a parallelogram from two adjacent sides and the included angle |
| Conditions | sides from 3 cm to 8 cm to the nearest half centimetre; the angle a whole number of degrees from 45° to 135°; compasses and a straight edge marked in centimetres, with a protractor used only to set the angle; response: the construction |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Construct parallelogram ABCD with AB = 5 cm, AD = 3.5 cm and ∠A = 60°. |
