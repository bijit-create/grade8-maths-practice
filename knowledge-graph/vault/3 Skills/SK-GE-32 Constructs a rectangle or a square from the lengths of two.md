# SK-GE-32

> Constructs a rectangle or a square from the lengths of two adjacent sides with compasses and a straight edge.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M815 Constructs different quadrilaterals using compasses]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | constructs |
| Content | a rectangle, or a square when the two sides are equal, from two adjacent sides |
| Conditions | sides from 3 cm to 8 cm to the nearest half centimetre; compasses and a straight edge marked in centimetres; response: the construction with the arcs of the perpendiculars left visible |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Construct a rectangle with adjacent sides 5 cm and 4 cm. Construct a square of side 4.5 cm. |

| Stopping rule | Drawing the base, the two perpendiculars and marking the opposite side is one sequence; a square is the same sequence with equal sides. |
