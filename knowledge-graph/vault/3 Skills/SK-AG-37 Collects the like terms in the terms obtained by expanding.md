# SK-AG-37

> Collects the like terms in the terms obtained by expanding a product of two polynomials.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M807 Multiplies algebraic expressions. E.g. Expands (2x-5)]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | collects |
| Content | the like terms of an expanded product |
| Conditions | four to nine terms in one or two variables with powers up to 3, as in 6x² + 14x − 15x − 35 and a³ + 3a² + 5a + 7a² + 21a + 35; coefficients integers from −45 to 45 or fractions with denominator up to 10; includes like terms written in a different order, as xy and yx, and pairs that cancel to zero; written symbols; answer with no like terms left |
| Role | step; carried out inside [[SK-AG-35 Expands the product of two binomials]] (sometimes), [[SK-AG-36 Expands the product of a binomial and a trinomial]] (always) |
| Example task | Collect the like terms in 6x² + 14x − 15x − 35.   Collect the like terms in x³ − x²y + xy² + x²y − xy² + y³. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Stopping rule | Grouping the like terms and adding their coefficients are done in one pass; the grouping alone gives a part that cannot be evidenced apart from the adding. |
