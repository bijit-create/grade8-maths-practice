# SK-NN-21

> Finds the square root of a decimal number that is a perfect square by the division method.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M804 Finds squares, cubes and square roots and cube roots]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the square root of a decimal number |
| Conditions | decimal numbers with two decimal places that are the squares of numbers with one decimal place, such as 2.56, 51.84 and 42.25, with up to two digits before the decimal point; by the division method with pairs on both sides of the decimal point; answer a decimal with one decimal place |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Find the square root of 51.84 by the division method. |
