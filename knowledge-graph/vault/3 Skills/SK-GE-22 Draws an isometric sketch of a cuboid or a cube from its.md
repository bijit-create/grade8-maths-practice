# SK-GE-22

> Draws an isometric sketch of a cuboid or a cube from its length, breadth and height on isometric dot paper.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M813 Represents 3D shapes on a plane surface such as sheet]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | draws |
| Content | an isometric sketch of a cuboid or a cube |
| Conditions | length, breadth and height each a whole number of dot spaces from 1 to 6; isometric dot paper; response: a sketch with the twelve edges drawn |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Draw an isometric sketch of a cuboid with length 5 units, breadth 3 units and height 2 units on isometric dot paper. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Statement | Pass: one principal verb. |
| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-GE-21 Draws an oblique sketch of a cuboid or a cube from its]]. A learner draws the edges of a 5 × 3 × 2 cuboid along the vertical and slanting dot lines with the right number of dot spaces, but in an oblique sketch draws the front face as a parallelogram instead of a rectangle. |
| Stopping rule | The three sets of edges are drawn along the dot lines in one sequence; no smaller task shows the representation. |
