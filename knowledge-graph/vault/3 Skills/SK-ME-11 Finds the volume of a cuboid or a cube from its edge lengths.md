# SK-ME-11

> Finds the volume of a cuboid or a cube from its edge lengths.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M818 Finds surface area and volume of cuboidal and]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the volume of a cuboid or a cube |
| Conditions | length, width and height given in the same unit, cm or m, whole numbers or one decimal place; a cube given by one edge; response: volume with cubic units (cm³ or m³) |
| Role | outcome task; carried out inside [[SK-ME-14 Solves a problem from a real-life situation that needs the]] (sometimes) |
| Example task | A box is 12 cm long, 8 cm wide and 5 cm high. Find its volume. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Statement | Pass: one principal verb. |
| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-ME-12 Finds the total surface area of a cuboid or a cube from its]]. A learner finds the volume of the box as 480 cm³, but gives its surface area as 12 × 8 + 8 × 5 + 12 × 5 = 196 cm², counting each pair of opposite faces once. |
| Stopping rule | A cube is a cuboid with equal edges, so cubes and cuboids give one task type. |
