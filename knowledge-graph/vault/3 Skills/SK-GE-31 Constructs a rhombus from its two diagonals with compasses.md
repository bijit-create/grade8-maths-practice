# SK-GE-31

> Constructs a rhombus from its two diagonals with compasses and a straight edge.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M815 Constructs different quadrilaterals using compasses]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | constructs |
| Content | a rhombus from two diagonals |
| Conditions | diagonals from 4 cm to 10 cm to the nearest tenth of a centimetre; compasses and a straight edge marked in centimetres; response: the construction with the arcs of the perpendicular bisector left visible |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Construct a rhombus whose diagonals are 5.2 cm and 6.4 cm. |
