# SK-GE-21

> Draws an oblique sketch of a cuboid or a cube from its length, breadth and height on squared paper.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M813 Represents 3D shapes on a plane surface such as sheet]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | draws |
| Content | an oblique sketch of a cuboid or a cube |
| Conditions | length, breadth and height each a whole number of squares from 1 to 6; squared paper; response: a sketch with the twelve edges drawn |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Draw an oblique sketch of a cuboid with length 4 squares, breadth 3 squares and height 2 squares on squared paper. |

## Grain tests (V2 §3.2)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Statement | Pass: one principal verb. |
| Task | Pass: the example task produces evidence of this skill. |
| Separation | Pass against [[SK-GE-22 Draws an isometric sketch of a cuboid or a cube from its]]. A learner draws the oblique sketch of a 4 × 3 × 2 cuboid correctly, but on isometric dot paper draws the front face as a rectangle with a right angle at each corner. |
| Stopping rule | The front face, the back face and the joining edges are drawn in one sequence; no smaller task shows the representation. |
