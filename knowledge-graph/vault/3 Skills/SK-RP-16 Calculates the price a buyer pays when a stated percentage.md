# SK-RP-16

> Calculates the price a buyer pays when a stated percentage of sales tax is added to a price.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M809 Applies the concept of percent in profit and loss]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | calculates |
| Content | price including sales tax |
| Conditions | price up to ₹50,000; tax rate a whole-number percent, called VAT in the outcome and GST in current use |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | A bag costs ₹1,800 and VAT is 12%. How much does the buyer pay? |
