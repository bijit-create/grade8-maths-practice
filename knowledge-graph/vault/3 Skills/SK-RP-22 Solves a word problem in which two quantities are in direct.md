# SK-RP-22

> Solves a word problem in which two quantities are in direct proportion, finding a missing value.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M810 Solves problems based on direct and inverse proportions]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | solves |
| Content | a direct proportion problem |
| Conditions | situation in words or a table; whole numbers or decimals up to two places; one missing value |
