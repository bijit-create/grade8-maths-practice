# SK-NN-06

> Justifies the divisibility rule of 2 or 5 for a two-digit or three-digit number from its generalised form.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M803 Proves divisibility rules of 2, 3,4, 5, 6, 9 and 11]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | justifies |
| Content | why a number is divisible by 2 or by 5 exactly when its units digit is |
| Conditions | two-digit number ab or three-digit number abc given by its digits, not in generalised form; rule stated in the task (the units digit decides); response in words or in letters |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Using the digits a, b and c of a three-digit number abc, show why abc is divisible by 5 only when c is 0 or 5. |
