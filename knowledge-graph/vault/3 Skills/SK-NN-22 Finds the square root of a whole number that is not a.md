# SK-NN-22

> Finds the square root of a whole number that is not a perfect square, to one decimal place, by the division method.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M804 Finds squares, cubes and square roots and cube roots]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the square root of a whole number that is not a perfect square |
| Conditions | whole numbers from 2 to 999 that are not perfect squares; by the division method continued to two decimal places by bringing down pairs of zeros, then rounded to one decimal place; answer a decimal with one decimal place |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | Find the square root of 7 to one decimal place by the division method. |
