# SK-NN-32

> Finds how many times one quantity is as large as another from their standard forms.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M805 Solves problems with integral exponents]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the quotient of two quantities given in standard form |
| Conditions | two positive quantities in the same unit, each given as a × 10^n with a from 1 to 9.999 and n an integer from -20 to 15; the quotient of the numbers a rounded to one decimal place; answer to the nearest whole number or as a × 10^n; contexts of length, mass and distance |
| Role | outcome task |
| Example task | The diameter of the Sun is 1.4 × 10^9 m and the diameter of the Earth is 1.2756 × 10^7 m. How many times as large as the Earth's diameter is the Sun's diameter? |
