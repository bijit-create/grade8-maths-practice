# SK-RP-21

> Decides whether two quantities in a situation are in direct proportion, in inverse proportion, or neither.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M810 Solves problems based on direct and inverse proportions]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | decides |
| Content | the kind of variation between two quantities |
| Conditions | situation in words or a table of three to five pairs; response with a reason |
| Role | step; carried out inside [[SK-RP-22 Solves a word problem in which two quantities are in direct]] (always), [[SK-RP-23 Solves a word problem in which two quantities are in]] (always) |
| Example task | The number of workers and the days to build a wall: direct, inverse, or neither? |
