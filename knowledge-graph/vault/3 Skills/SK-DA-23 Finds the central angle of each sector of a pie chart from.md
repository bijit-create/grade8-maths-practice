# SK-DA-23

> Finds the central angle of each sector of a pie chart from the values or percentages in a table.

Authored by ConveGenius. Belongs to [[LO-M819 Draws and interprets bar charts and pie charts]].

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | finds |
| Content | the central angle of a sector of a pie chart |
| Conditions | 3 to 6 categories; whole-number values whose total divides 360 (such as 12, 24, 30, 36, 40, 60, 72, 90), or whole-number percentages that give whole-number angles; written working as a fraction of 360°; angles checked to add up to 360° |
| Role | step; carried out inside [[SK-DA-22 Draws a pie chart of a data set from a table, with each]] (always) |
| Example task | Priya spends 8 hours sleeping, 6 at school, 4 on homework, 2 at play and 4 on other things in a day of 24 hours. Find the central angle of each sector. |
