## Current teaching decisions

1. **Prepare the relevant knowledge, then integrate it.** The skill's actual requirements determine which KIs need preparation. A missing unrelated KI does not stop eligible work elsewhere. Known relevant parts may be retrieved briefly; unknown relevant parts receive teaching and checks. A short supported complete task may connect those parts while the skill is developing. “All KIs first” means all relevant KIs for that performance, not every KI in a chapter or graph.
2. **Core teaching supports the learning process.** It is authored explanation, modelling, meaningful comparison, prediction/completion, guided practice, feedback, fading, whole-task use and later retrieval as appropriate. A catalogue tag, right-answer counter or answer reveal alone is not teaching.
3. **Adapt to the response, with uncertainty.** A wrong or unreadable response opens a short check of access or the most relevant competing explanations. Teach the indicated part, give fresh practice and return to a fresh whole task. If the evidence stays ambiguous, preserve “not yet known”, provide usable support and save a next step. Do not invent a cause.
4. **Increase independence and rigour on evidence.** Honour Bijit's 15 Nov decision: harder skill work follows practice and development of that skill. Do not impose a universal slow route; established skills can receive varied, integrated or harder work while specific gaps receive preparation. Same-day success allows a provisional move with later verification, not a permanent mastery declaration.
5. **“Held” is scoped.** State what she did, on which kinds, with which supports, and after what delay. Retain evidence when later work fails; revise the claim. Unknown is neither failed nor mastered. Legacy “passed” remains a historical interface state unless its underlying evidence supports a stronger claim.

## Decision record, not theory labels

For a material teaching choice record: target and current learner evidence → plausible options → relevant principles with their role (constraint, guide, trade-off or evidence requirement) → chosen action and why → expected discriminating evidence → what would make us revise it. Include source/read depth and important applicability limits. Small routine continuations need only a short reason in the plan/evidence record; do not write an essay for each item.

Current reasons: delayed mixed work checks a missing kind of evidence; sign-sensitive examples distinguish omission of a sign from other explanations; relevant-prerequisite preparation reduces avoidable load while whole-task practice establishes coordination. Revise when fresh independent performance, access problems, support dependence or delayed results contradict these expectations.
