Grade 8 Maths

Choose a page. Each page keeps your place on this device, so you can stop and come back to it later.

Equations

Find what a letter stands for, and decide what is an expression

Start here. You find what number a letter such as x stands for, and you decide whether a line of numbers, letters and signs is an expression.

Split an expression into terms, and find the coefficient of the letter

Do this after the page on letters and expressions. You split an expression into its terms, and you find the number that multiplies the letter in each term.

Quadrilaterals

Make a quadrilateral from 4 points

Start the quadrilaterals here, after the equations pages. You tap points in turn to make a closed shape with 4 straight sides.

Decide whether each shape is a quadrilateral

Do this after the page where you make a quadrilateral. You look at one shape at a time and decide whether it is a quadrilateral.

Draw the diagonals of a quadrilateral

Do this after the page where you decide whether each shape is a quadrilateral. You tap two vertices to draw a line, and you draw the diagonals of each quadrilateral.

Find out what the 4 angles of a quadrilateral add up to

Do this after the page on diagonals. A diagonal cuts a quadrilateral into 2 triangles, and you use the 2 triangles to find what the 4 angles add up to. You also drag a vertex to change the shape and add up its angles again.

Find the missing angle of a quadrilateral

Do this after the page on what the 4 angles add up to. You are given 3 angles of a quadrilateral, and you find the fourth angle.

Find an angle of a quadrilateral from the angle beside it

Do this after the page on the missing angle. One side of the quadrilateral goes on past a vertex in a straight line, and you find the angle inside the quadrilateral from the angle outside it.

Find the angles of a quadrilateral when they are written with x

Do this after the page on the missing angle. The angles are written with x, such as x, 2x, 3x and 4x. You write an equation, find x and then find the angles.
