# Charter: what this work is responsible for (Student 2)

Adopted 11 Nov 2026 from Student 1's charter (7 Nov, updated 10 Nov), goals only. His evidence is left out; there is no record of hers yet. `CLAUDE.md` turns these goals into design rules and wins where the two differ on how to design or read evidence.

## The problem

> How can we design and operate teaching that enables her to build connected mathematical knowledge, use it to perform increasingly complex skills, retain it, and apply it independently, including on paper, while the system itself provides the guidance, practice, feedback and adjustment needed to get her there?

The system takes responsibility for the whole route from what she can already do to successful learning, **without an adult beside her**. What she can already do is to be found from her own dated work, not assumed.

## Success: what has become available to her

For each mathematical goal (the first is linear equations in one variable, NCERT M806), seven connected dimensions, each with its own evidence:

| Dimension | What she can do |
|---|---|
| Meaning | Show what the quantities, symbols and relationships mean, by writing, examples or drawings |
| Execution | Carry out the calculation or procedure accurately |
| Selection | Recognise when to use it, including when problem types are mixed |
| Integration | Combine it with other knowledge to solve a larger problem |
| Retention | Use it after a gap, before any refresher |
| Transfer | Use it with unfamiliar numbers, wording or presentation, including the intended paper format |
| Independence | Start, proceed, check and recover using the resources permitted, without an adult directing her |

Reciting a rule does not establish applying it; carrying out a familiar procedure does not establish choosing it; passing the parts does not establish combining them. These are evaluation questions, not seven modules or gates; one task may give evidence on several.

## The unit of design: a complete performance with a planned route

Each unit specifies: the complete performance wanted (for example "solve an equation with the unknown on both sides and check it"); the knowledge it builds on; the new relationships or actions to establish; the teaching experiences and what she is expected to notice, connect, retrieve or do in each; how support and task complexity change, and on what evidence; how retention and independent use will be shown; what happens when the expected progress does not occur.

## Six processes the system must manage

1. **Shared understanding of the task.** Wording, notation, picture and answer controls communicate one task. Unfamiliar conventions (the balance, the box for the unknown) are taught.
2. **Making the intended thinking possible and worthwhile.** Every picture and interaction has a stated job. A picture is faded on evidence that she can use its connection to the mathematics.
3. **Parts and their coordination.** Focused practice where needed, immediate use of what is available, and repeated return to the whole performance.
4. **Engagement as part of instruction.** The purpose is clear, feedback is usable, help and breaks are available, and engagement with the mathematics is told apart from engagement with the screen.
5. **Learning that survives the lesson.** Retrieval across sessions, varied use, return after gaps, support that changes as she gains capability.
6. **Improving its own decisions.** A record that answers "under these conditions, this teaching was followed by this improvement, or did not produce it", never "secure", "weak" or an invented attention score.

The failure sequence to design against: an ambiguous task → an unexpected answer → feedback that misses her reading → less purposeful attempts → read as a mathematical gap → more mismatched teaching.

## Measurement

Every answer is kept with its conditions: the item, the support given (`supplied`), the response, the reply or retry, and later fresh or delayed performance. A task that allowed two readings is a fault in the task and is fixed. A fast answer, or one that matches a number on the screen, is not discarded. Elapsed time is not evidence of attention or struggle. One day's success is performance; learning shows across sessions and formats.

## The standard

Translate research into conditional decisions (what kind of knowledge; what process the teaching supports; under what conditions the principle applies; what it looks like here; what later performance would show it helped), and revise them against her later work. The design is a proposal; the evidence is what she can then write, solve, remember and learn next.
